Formulas

  • Horizontal velocity: vx = v₀ · cosθ
  • Vertical velocity: vy = v₀ · sinθ
  • Time to apex: t = vy / g
  • Max height: h = h₀ + vy² / (2g)
  • Time of flight: from quadratic solution of y(t)=0
  • Range: R = vx · total time
  • Final velocity: v = √(vx² + vyf²)
